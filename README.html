Sphere Packing Lattices

New dense packings in dimensions 67–71 from Nebe's 72-dimensional lattice, and a search for an extremal 96-dimensional lattice built from the Leech lattice.

Benjamin John Schulz · computations October 12, 2026 · Python (NumPy, SymPy, python-flint, fpylll, NetworkX)

Status in one line. Verified antipode packings beating every possible cross-section of Γ72 in dimensions 67–71; no extremal 96-dimensional lattice yet, but several construction families are now ruled out with proofs and explicit certificates.

1. Antipode packings from Γ72 (dimensions 66–71)

For a saturated rank-k cross-section K of the extremal lattice Γ72 (minimum 8, determinant 1) and a cluster S ⊂ K* of squared diameter β, stacking |S| shifted copies of the orthogonal slice gives a packing in dimension 72 − k with center density

δ = |S| · det(K)^(-1/2) · ((8 − β)/4)^((72−k)/2)

(Conway–Sloane 1996; Chen et al. 2025, Thm 1). The ceiling column is the Hermite bound on the best any cross-section of Γ72 could do.

Every certificate is checked exactly: saturation via Smith normal form, an explicit integer vector of Γ72 realizing each translate, rational β, and density. Relies on min(Γ72) = 8 (Nebe 2012). The standard tables (Nebe–Sloane, Cohn) list no packings in dimensions 65–71; stacking layers on Nebe's Ne64 was not evaluated and could compete in dimension 67.

2. Toward an extremal 96-dimensional lattice

An extremal even unimodular lattice in dimension 96 would have minimum norm 10. Whether one exists is open (rank 88 was settled in Sept 2026).

Automorphism screen

From the standard lemma on prime-order automorphisms (fixed lattice of determinant ps, s ≤ min(dim F, k)) with Hermite/Blichfeldt bounds, an extremal 96-dimensional lattice has:

  • no automorphism of prime order 31 ≤ p ≤ 89 (in particular none of order 47);
  • order 97 only fixed-point-free (an ideal lattice in Q(ζ₉₇));
  • order 23 only with a 74-dimensional fixed lattice; order 29 only with 68;
  • order 19: fixed dimension 78 or 6; order 17: 80 or fixed-point-free; order 13: 84, 72, 12 or fixed-point-free.

Quick start

pip install numpy sympy python-flint fpylll networkx

# Γ72 antipode certificates (exact)
cd gamma72_antipode
python3 verify.py gamma72.json certs.json out.json
python3 verify.py gamma72.json improved_66_certificate.json out66.json

# Leech96: twist obstruction certificates (needs leech23.json, cands.json)
cd ../leech96_hermitian
python3 kill_cert.py . cands.json kill_certs_rerun.json

# Symmetric Paley family: explicit kills from the order-47 fixed sublattice
cd ../leech96_symmetry_screen
python3 fixedkill.py paley_sym.json fixedkill_rerun.json
python3 aut_screen.py

# Z16 candidate: structure check + BKZ (block ≤ 40) and mod-4 layer certificate
cd ../z16_candidate_checks
python3 z16_check_and_kill.py candidate_3cycle.json 2 40 kill_rerun.json
python3 layer4.py candidate_3cycle.json layer4_rerun.json

fpylll wheels may ship without strategy files; the scripts avoid them (unpruned BKZ, direct enumeration).

What is and is not claimed

  • Proved / exactly certified: the Γ72 packings above (given min Γ72 = 8); the Q(√−23) and small-discriminant obstructions; the kills of all 24 Hermitian candidates and all 53 symmetric Paley lattices; the mod-4 layer of the Z16 candidate; the automorphism screen (given the standard lemma).
  • Heuristic only: BKZ non-detection of short vectors; the Q(√−11) genus walk.
  • Not claimed: absolute record status in dimensions 65–71; existence or nonexistence of an extremal 96-dimensional lattice.
