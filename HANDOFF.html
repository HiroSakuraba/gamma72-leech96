  • No published records exist for dims 65–71 in the Nebe–Sloane or Cohn tables. Layering on Ne64 (2^25.36 in dim 64) was not evaluated; dim 67 is the claim most exposed to that comparison.

Leads, ranked

  1. E₈ ⊗ J₂₄ over Z[(1+√−7)/2]. Class number 1, split norm 2 × 5 = 10, order-7 symmetry allowed by the screen. Requires an extremal 7-modular 24-dim lattice J with Hermitian structure; its theta series 1 + 84672q⁵ + 91728q⁶ + … is admissible. Steps: build a √−7 structure on E₈ (Gauss sum over an order-7 Weyl element, as done for Leech), search/construct J, kill-test rank 2–4 tensors.
  2. Ideal lattices in Q(ζ₉₇) (fixed-point-free order 97 allowed). Analogue of Nebe's "large automorphism" classification for 72. Needs class groups/units — Magma or PARI on a bigger machine.
  3. Fixed-point-free order 13 or 17 Hermitian lattices over Z[ζ₁₃] (rank 8) or Z[ζ₁₇] (rank 6), in the style of Sakamoto's rank 88.
  4. Nonexistence attempt: compute Kominers' uniquely determined degree-2 Siegel theta series for rank 96 and test coefficient positivity/integrality.
  5. Z16 candidate: extend layer certificates to 2-mod-4 and odd layers; BKZ ≥ 50 on a larger machine.

3. Gotchas learned this session

BKZ lies by omission. Twice, lattices that "survived" BKZ-30/40 had norm-4–8 vectors hiding in small structured sublattices (ideal twists; automorphism fixed lattices). Always run the structural checks (twist pairing, fixed sublattice of every known automorphism, automorphism screen) before trusting a survivor.
  • fpylll strategy files missing in the pip wheel: BKZ.DEFAULT_STRATEGY and SVP.shortest_vector fail. Use unpruned BKZ and svp_util.py (direct enumeration).
  • fpylll Gram-mode LLL (GSO.INT_GRAM) returned a non-reduced transform; use gram_lll.py for small Gram matrices.
  • SymPy HNF/det are too slow at 96×96 or 192×96; use python-flint (fmpz_mat.hnf(), .det()).
  • Integer overflow: neighbor-walk bases blow up; keep big ints in Python/FLINT until after LLL.
  • Killing background jobs: pkill -f <name> also matches the invoking shell; filter by comm == python3.
  • BKZ-45 on a 96-dim lattice takes ≈ 13 minutes on 2 cores; BKZ-50 did not finish a round in an hour.
