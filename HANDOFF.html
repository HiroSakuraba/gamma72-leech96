Handoff: Γ72 packings and the dimension-96 search

State of the work on October 12, 2026, for whoever picks it up next. The public overview is on the project page; the code is in HiroSakuraba/gamma72-leech96.

Dimensions 67–71 are done and exactly verified. Dimension 66 is improved but still below the cross-section ceiling. In dimension 96 there is no extremal lattice yet: four construction families are closed with proofs, one supplied candidate is open, and the leading route (E₈ ⊗ J₂₄ over Z[(1+√−7)/2]) has been narrowed but not resolved.

Carry forward

  • Everything assumes min(Γ72) = 8, Nebe's theorem; it was not re-enumerated.
  • The Gram matrix's three diagonal 24 × 24 blocks are √2·Leech (det 224, 196,560 vectors of norm 8, checked). Useful when searching for structured cross-sections.
  • No published packings exist in dimensions 65–71. Layering on Ne64 (225.36 in dimension 64) was not evaluated; dimension 67 is the claim most exposed.

Dimension 96

  • Closed by proof
    Leech tensor partner over Q(√−23)Class number 3: an ideal twist of Leech with roots pairs with the twisted partner to give norm ≤ 8. 24 explicit certificates. Also excluded by the automorphism filter (order 23 with an 8-dimensional fixed lattice).
  • Closed by proof
    Same over Q(√−d), d ≤ 8The partner's Hermitian minimum is at most √d, so split vectors have norm ≤ 8.
  • Closed by proof
    Symmetric Paley codes A = mC + cI over Z/NThe order-47 shift fixes a 4-dimensional sublattice with minimum ≤ 9.70, hence ≤ 8. 53 explicit vectors.
  • Closed by proof
    Any automorphism of prime order 31 to 89Automorphism filter in leech96_symmetry_screen/aut_screen.py.
  • Likely closed
    Partners over Q(√−11)Needs an extremal 8-dimensional 11-modular lattice with Hermitian structure. Theta series admissible, genus walk found none, and Scharlau–Schulze-Pillot (1998) is cited as saying the unique one lacks the structure; not re-checked here.
  • Open
    Z/16 Paley 3-cycle candidateEven unimodular, verified exactly. BKZ to block 45 finds minimum 10. Layers u ≡ 0 mod 8 and mod 4 are certified free of norm ≤ 8; odd and 2-mod-4 layers are open.

Leads, in order

  1. E₈ tensor J₂₄ over Z[(1+√−7)/2]. Class number 1, split norm 2 × 5 = 10, order 7 allowed by the filter. Needs an extremal 7-modular 24-dimensional J with Hermitian structure (theta 1 + 84672q⁵ + 91728q⁶ + … is admissible). Done: two √−7 structures on E₈; Leech = Pb ⊗ E₈ with explicit √−7. Searched, nothing extremal: 7-modular sublattices π⁻¹(C) of Leech (random, hill-climb, order-3-invariant: best ≈ 1,705 short pairs), Kneser 2-neighbours (no progress), and a complete search of ideal lattices in Q(ζ₃₅), Q(ζ₅₆), Q(ζ₈₄) (none in ζ₃₅; minimum 8 at best otherwise; PARI, GRH). Next: Hermitian lattices of rank > 1 over Z[ζ₇], Z[ζ₂₁] or Z[ζ₂₈] (fixed-point-free order 7 or 21 is still possible); a neighbour walk with a smarter score. Details in e8_j24/README.md.
  2. Ideal lattices in Q(ζ₉₇). Fixed-point-free order 97 is allowed. The analogue of Nebe's large-automorphism classification in dimension 72; needs class groups and units in Magma or PARI on a larger machine.
  3. Fixed-point-free order 13 or 17: Hermitian lattices over Z[ζ₁₃] of rank 8 or Z[ζ₁₇] of rank 6, in the style of Sakamoto's rank 88.
  4. Nonexistence attempt: compute Kominers' uniquely determined degree-2 Siegel theta series in rank 96 and test its coefficients for positivity and integrality.
  5. Z/16 candidate: extend the layer certificates; run BKZ at block 50 or more on a larger machine.

Things that went wrong, so they don't again

Lattice reduction misses structured short vectors. Twice, lattices that survived BKZ-30 or BKZ-40 had vectors of norm 4 to 8 hiding in small sublattices: ideal twists, and fixed sublattices of automorphisms. Run the structural checks (twist pairing, the fixed sublattice of every known automorphism, the automorphism filter) before trusting a survivor.

  • The pip build of fpylll ships without its strategy files, so BKZ.DEFAULT_STRATEGY and SVP.shortest_vector fail. Use unpruned BKZ and svp_util.py.
  • fpylll's Gram-mode LLL (GSO.INT_GRAM) returned a transform that was not reduced; gram_lll.py handles small Gram matrices exactly.
  • SymPy's Hermite normal form and determinant are too slow at 96 × 96; python-flint's fmpz_mat.hnf() and .det() are fast.
  • Neighbor-walk bases grow quickly; keep integers in Python or FLINT until after reduction to avoid int64 overflow.
  • pkill -f name also matches the shell that runs it; select processes by comm == python3.
  • BKZ-45 on a 96-dimensional lattice takes about 13 minutes on two cores; BKZ-50 did not finish a round in an hour.
